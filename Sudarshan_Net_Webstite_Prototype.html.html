About the founder

Built by a curious learner

Sudarshan Net is a student-led venture exploring how India's intellectual traditions can meet modern technology.

Rishaan Anand Srivastava

Rishaan is a Year 10 student at GD Goenka Public School, Lucknow, with a strong interest in Indian history and Sanatan Dharma alongside his academic studies in Business Studies and Economics. He is also a staunch Shiva bhakta and devotee of Lord Shiva; this personal devotion is an important part of his connection to Sanatani traditions.

The venture

Sudarshan Net was started by Rishaan to bring timeless Sanatani wisdom into the modern era through digital learning, interactive educational tools, research and technology, with the hope that society can put this knowledge to good use.
